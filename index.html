Selected work

Two very different kinds of software.

01

Full-stack platform · Next.js, PostgreSQL, Stripe

Rebuilding an affiliate marketing platform on real financial rails

Led a ground-up rebuild of a two-app affiliate marketing platform for a sports betting / iGaming business — an internal admin console and a partner-facing portal, both reading from one shared source of truth.

  • Migrated the system off a fragile, spreadsheet-backed data layer onto PostgreSQL, closing real security gaps (hardcoded credentials, missing authentication) in the process.
  • Designed a canonical financial calculation kernel shared by both apps, so margin and earnings math can never silently drift between what staff sees and what a partner sees.
  • Shipped a live Stripe Connect payout workflow — performance entry, period close, approval, and real bank transfers — then built self-service onboarding so partners connect their own Stripe account directly, no staff hand-off required.
  • Rebuilt partner-facing account management, brand requests, and real-time performance reporting end to end.
02

Mobile · Flutter, Supabase, iOS & Android

Happy Camper — a campsite discovery product, from schema to paywall

Built a Flutter application for discovering and exploring campsites across Argentina, on contract for a client. I owned the whole vertical slice: the database and its migrations, the domain model, the map, and the paid tier on top of it.

  • Structured the app on a strict clean-architecture split — domain, data and feature layers — with dependency injection and Bloc for state, so the business rules never depend on Flutter or on Supabase.
  • Designed the Supabase backend across three migrations, one of them a dedicated security pass that tightened access rules on the initial model rather than leaving them to be discovered later.
  • Built an interactive map over the full campsite catalogue with clustered markers, backed by a normalisation layer for inconsistent source data and local caching so the app stays useful with no signal — which is exactly where you are when you're looking for a campsite.
  • Shipped the parts that make it a product rather than a demo: B2C subscriptions and a paywall, a recommendation engine, a gamification layer of ranks and photo-based rewards, and full Spanish and English localisation.

About

How I actually work.

I move between mobile and full-stack platform work, but the throughline is the same: I'd rather spend an extra day on data integrity and a shared source of truth than ship something that's "probably correct." When a product touches real payouts, real user accounts, or real production data, that difference is the whole job.

Most recently that's meant rebuilding a financial platform's backbone — input validation, atomic transactions, a canonical calculation model shared across two apps — and shipping it alongside the visible features partners actually use. Building in Flutter taught me to care just as much about what a user experiences in the moment: responsive UI, clean architecture, and the kind of state management that doesn't fall over under real usage.
